# Privacy Policy

## 1. Data Protection at a Glance
### General Information
The following notes provide a simple overview of what happens to your personal data when you visit this website. Personal data is any data with which you can be personally identified.

## 2. Hosting and Content Delivery Networks (CDN)
### External Hosting (Vercel)
This website is hosted by an external service provider, Vercel Inc. (340 S Lemon Ave #4133, Walnut, CA 91789, USA). Personal data collected on this website is stored on the host's servers.

### Cloudflare (CDN & DNS)
We use services provided by Cloudflare Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) to secure and accelerate the delivery of our website. Cloudflare processes technical network data (including visitors' IP addresses) to fend off attacks and provide content.

## 3. Map Tiles (Basemap Providers)
### CARTO
The interactive map loads its background map images ("tiles") from CARTO (CartoDB Inc., USA). To deliver the tiles, your browser connects directly to CARTO's servers, which receive your IP address and technical request data (e.g. browser type, requested map area). This is necessary to display the map (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in a functioning map). Privacy policy: https://carto.com/privacy

### Esri (optional)
If you switch the basemap to "Esri Dark Canvas" in the map settings, tiles are loaded from Esri (Environmental Systems Research Institute, Inc., USA) instead, with the same kind of technical data. Privacy policy: https://www.esri.com/en-us/privacy/overview

All other scripts and styles of this website are served from our own domain; no further third-party CDNs are used.

## 4. Usage Statistics (Vercel Web Analytics)
To see how many people visit this website and which parts are used, we use Vercel Web Analytics, a service of Vercel Inc. (340 S Lemon Ave #4133, Walnut, CA 91789, USA). It works without cookies and does not store anything on your device.

For each page view, the following is recorded: time, page address, the page you came from (referrer), country/region, browser, operating system and device type (desktop, tablet or mobile). Visitors are not identified individually: Vercel only uses a hash derived from the request, which is discarded after 24 hours, so visits cannot be linked across days or to a person. We only see the results as aggregated statistics.

Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in anonymous usage statistics to improve the website). Privacy information: https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy

## 5. Local Storage in Your Browser
This website does not set cookies and does not track you across websites. If you change the basemap settings, your choice (and an optional CARTO key you enter) is saved in your browser's local storage so it persists between visits. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time via your browser settings.

## 6. General Information and Mandatory Information
### SSL/TLS Encryption
For security reasons and to protect the transmission of confidential content, this site uses SSL/TLS encryption. You can recognize an encrypted connection in your browser's address bar when it changes from "http://" to "https://" and by the lock icon in your browser bar.

### Email Contact
If you contact us via email (e.g., at contact@theworldsresources.org), your provided email address and the data transmitted will be stored by us for the purpose of processing your request. We will not share this data without your consent.
