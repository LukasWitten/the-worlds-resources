Data Sources & Transparency

“The World’s Resources” is committed to full data openness. All geocoordinates, pipeline routes and production volumes come from verified official reports and scientific open-data initiatives.

Bundesnetzagentur (BNetzA) & FNB Gas

Monitoring reports and network infrastructure data on gas transmission pipelines, LNG terminals and underground storage facilities.

bundesnetzagentur.de ↗

U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) & BGR

Global commodity statistics, extraction volumes and world reserve estimates for lithium, metals and critical minerals.

usgs.gov ↗

Statistisches Bundesamt (Destatis)

Official foreign-trade statistics on commodity import volumes, countries of origin and energy carriers.

destatis.de ↗

OpenStreetMap & Overpass API

Exact topographic geocoordinates of port facilities, refinery sites and pipeline corridors.

openstreetmap.org ↗
