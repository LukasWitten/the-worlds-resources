Data Sources & Transparency

"The World’s Resources" is committed to absolute data transparency. All geographic coordinates, transport routes, and statistical figures presented on the map are gathered exclusively from open and verified public institutions.

Statistisches Bundesamt (Destatis)

National trade volumes, import statistics, and raw material processing metrics for Germany.

Bundesnetzagentur (BNetzA)

Infrastructure data regarding energy grids, pipelines, and domestic resource distribution networks.

Global Energy Monitor & OpenStreetMap

Geographic coordinates for extraction facilities, refineries, and major supply line waypoints.
